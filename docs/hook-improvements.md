# Hook Improvement Playbook — "Guess the Band"

> Goal: lift the **0–3s retention** of `tiktok_reels.mp4` (the single biggest lever on reach),
> using **visual/motion hooks** — not static text. Every idea here is designed to drop into the
> existing automated pipeline (Claude → fal.ai → ffmpeg → Telegram) with **zero manual editing**.

---

## What the research says (the rules we're designing against)

The first **0–3 seconds decide everything** — swipe-away clusters at 2–3s, and videos that hold
**>65–70% at 3s get 4–7× more impressions**. The winning hook formula is:

**`Pattern Break` + `Emotional Trigger` + `Curiosity Gap`**

Applied to a *guessing game*, four mechanics matter most:

1. **Open loop / curiosity gap** — pose the unanswered question in frame 1; the brain stays to close it.
2. **Micro-dopamine quiz loop** — "try to answer before the timer" earns watch-time + comments.
3. **Comment bait** — intentional "mistakes," polarizing claims, and "tag someone who…" drive the
   comments that the algorithm rewards.
4. **Audio is half the hook** — especially on TikTok, the *sound* in the first second is weighted heavily.
   Our hook is currently near-silent; that's leaving reach on the table.

Text rule from the data: **one claim, ≤7 words, animated** (kinetic text beats static).

Sources: see bottom of doc.

---

## Current hook (baseline)

`build_hook_cmd()` in `scripts/generate_video.py`, `HOOK_DUR = 3.0`:
blurred 2×3 collage → brand text drops in → hook line scale-pops → cyan underline → sub line.
It's clean but **static and silent** — three stacked text reveals over a still blurred grid.
That's exactly the "slapping text on" pattern the research warns against.

Budget note: total video ≈ **62.3s**. We clear the 60s monetization floor with ~2.3s of slack,
so hooks that *add* 1–2s are fine (they actually push us more safely over 60s).

## The 5 ideas (cheap/fast → ambitious)

### 1. Rapid-fire "smash-cut" pre-roll (1.2s) — *pattern break, $0*
Before the title card, flash all 6 puzzle images in **~0.2s each** with alternating
punch-zoom / whip-pan and a beat-synced *thwack* on every cut, ending frozen on a half-revealed
image + **"GUESS ALL 6?"**. It's a visual machine-gun that breaks the scroll pattern and front-loads
the curiosity gap (you see there's a game before you read a word).

- **Why it works:** maximal pattern interrupt + motion in frame 1; previews the payoff (6 puzzles).
- **Build:** pure ffmpeg. We already have the 6 images. Chain 6 short `zoompan`/`crop` segments via
  the existing xfade/concat machinery; add a `scratch.wav`/`thwack` per cut in the audio mux.
- **Cost:** $0. **Effort:** ~half a day in `build_hook_cmd`. **Risk:** low.
- **Scales:** entirely data-driven by `len(images)` (already dynamic).

### 2. The "decoy / wrong answer" false-start — *comment bait, $0*
Open mid-game on the **hardest** band's image with a big red struck-through wrong guess
(`✗ NOT COLDPLAY`) or a stat hook (`98% GET #4 WRONG`). The deliberate "mistake" + impossible
challenge bait people into the comments to correct/argue.

- **Why it works:** intentional-mistake comment bait + curiosity gap + social-proof stat. Comments
  are the strongest ranking signal we can manufacture.
- **Build:** ffmpeg `drawtext` over the existing image. The "wrong guess" can be generated by Claude
  in the same content call (add a `decoy_guess` field per band — a plausible-but-wrong band name).
  Difficulty data already exists to pick the hardest one.
- **Cost:** ~$0 (one extra Claude field). **Effort:** small. **Risk:** low; A/B the stat vs the decoy.
- **Scales:** fully automated — Claude already returns structured JSON.

### 3. Recurring **animated mascot** host (2D), not a real person — *brand identity, low cost*
A signature branded character — e.g. a **vinyl-record-headed VJ / radio-head DJ** — "presents" the
game with a punchy TTS line. Cheaper, more consistent, and more *ownable* than a realistic avatar,
and a recurring character compounds **channel identity → follows → returning-viewer retention**.

- **Why it works:** a familiar face/character across videos builds a para-social hook ("oh it's THIS
  channel"), which lifts retention on *every future* video, not just one.
- **Build:** generate one reusable character (fal.ai / a rigged PNG set with a few mouth shapes).
  Per day: Claude writes a 1-line intro → TTS (e.g. the audio stack we already synthesize, or a cheap
  TTS API) → simple ffmpeg mouth-flap/head-bob (swap mouth frames on audio amplitude) composited over
  the collage. Or feed the still + line to an image-to-video/lip-sync model if we want fancier motion.
- **Cost:** ~cents/day (TTS) once the character exists. **Effort:** 1–2 days (rig + animation logic).
- **Scales:** one asset, infinite lines. This is the **best ROI of the "character" options.**

### 4. **AI avatar game-show host (HeyGen)** — *premium attention hijack* — your idea
A realistic human host delivers a 2–3s cold-open ("Only real music fans get all 6 — let's go"),
cut out and composited over the blurred collage like a TV game-show bumper. Use it **only for the
hook** (cost scales with seconds, so keep it short).

- **Why it works:** a real human face is the strongest scroll-stopper there is; it hijacks attention
  and frames the game with authority/energy a text card can't.
- **Build:** new Celery task → HeyGen API (template/batch-friendly, built for "mail-merge" video) or
  D-ID (clean REST, per-minute billing). Generate a 2–3s talking-head with transparent/greenscreen
  bg → ffmpeg `chromakey`/overlay onto our hook. Claude writes the line; same approval flow.
- **Cost (the honest math):**
  - **D-ID** ≈ **$5.90/min** → a 3s hook ≈ **~$0.30/video** → **~$9/month** at 1/day. Transparent, batch REST.
  - **HeyGen** PAYG from $5; Avatar III ≈ 3 credits/min → a 3s hook is a **fraction of a credit** (pennies);
    strongest batch/template tooling and lip-sync. (Captions AI has **no API** → not pipeline-viable.)
  - Either way: **trivial** vs. the upside. The real cost is integration + render latency, not $.
- **Effort:** 2–3 days (new task, API, chromakey composite, failure/fallback handling).
- **Scales:** yes, but it's an external API dependency + extra latency → make it **fail open** (skip the
  avatar and fall back to idea #1/#5 if the API errors, so the daily run never blocks).
- **Methodical caveat:** this is the highest-cost, highest-variance bet. **Validate cheap hooks first,**
  then A/B human-host vs. best non-avatar hook on real retention before committing the dependency.

### 5. Kinetic "stakes UI" hook — *fixes the static-text problem directly, $0*
Replace the three static text reveals with **kinetic typography + live game UI from frame 1**:
word-by-word pop synced to the beat, a `0/6` score counter, a difficulty meter, and the
**countdown timer already running** so the open loop is *visible*, not described. Personalized line
from Claude's existing `hook` field (e.g. "POV: you think you know music").

- **Why it works:** motion + an immediately-visible open loop (timer/score) > static claims; satisfies
  the "≤7 words, animated" rule and makes the *game* legible in 1 second.
- **Build:** pure ffmpeg — animate `drawtext` per-word (staggered alpha/scale we already do for the
  hook line), add a counter + meter as drawbox/drawtext. No new assets.
- **Cost:** $0. **Effort:** ~1 day. **Risk:** low. **Great default baseline** to A/B everything else against.

## Cross-cutting win: fix the **audio** hook (do this regardless)
Right now the hook is basically silent. Add a **0.5s audio sting** (riser/whoosh + bass hit) on the
first frame and a driving beat under the hook. On TikTok the first-second *sound* is weighted heavily;
this is a cheap, universal retention lift layered under any visual idea above.

---

## Recommended rollout (methodical, not random)

| Phase | Ship | Why first |
|------|------|-----------|
| 1 | **#5 kinetic stakes UI** + **audio sting** | $0, directly fixes "static text," becomes the A/B baseline |
| 2 | **#1 smash-cut pre-roll** | $0 pattern-break; biggest cheap swing at the 3s mark |
| 3 | **#2 decoy/comment-bait** | $0, targets comments (ranking signal), one Claude field |
| 4 | **#3 animated mascot** | builds durable channel identity; modest effort, owns the brand |
| 5 | **#4 HeyGen/D-ID host** | only after cheap hooks are validated; highest cost/dependency |

**Measure before you scale:** the metric that matters is **3-second view rate** (and comment rate for
#2). TikTok/IG analytics expose retention curves. Ship one change at a time, run each ~5–7 days,
keep the winner, stack the next. Don't bundle changes or you won't know what worked.

**Build it to A/B natively:** add a `HOOK_VARIANT` config (e.g. `kinetic | smashcut | decoy | mascot | avatar`)
to `generate_video.py` so we can rotate variants per day and attribute retention — turns the whole
channel into a continuous hook-testing machine, which is the real long-term unlock.
